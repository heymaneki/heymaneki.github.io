How the cat works

The cat up top is a live sample of the kind of small, polished thing I make. I directed it; AI coding agents (Claude Code and Codex) drew it, animated it and tested it. It is hand-written SVG, CSS and plain JavaScript inside this one page: no images, no libraries, no tracking.

  • Head: sinks when it naps, wiggles when it purrs
  • Left paw: beckons and drums
  • Right paw: drums
  • Eyes: blink, follow your cursor, close when happy

This wiring diagram is hooked up to the real cat. It moves when the cat moves.

Things to try

  • Type anything, or click an empty spot on the page. The cat drums along. Keep it up for a combo.
  • Shake your mouse over the cat, or press and hold it on a phone, to pet it.
  • Click the cat for a fortune.
  • Leave it alone for 30 seconds.
  • Turn on sound at the top. Every sound is generated live in code.
